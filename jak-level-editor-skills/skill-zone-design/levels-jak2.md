# Jak II: every level

Every level of Jak II the editor extracted (`library/jak2/<level>/`), 148 in all: 86 with decor of their own, the others loaded over them by the game (a mission's objects: 50; models only: 0; cutscenes: 3; characters for a scene: 0; menus and empty layers: 8).

Each row says what the level is and what sets it apart: the textures and objects it has that the other levels of its zone lack. Model for it with its zone's entry in [jak2.md](jak2.md). A level without decor of its own is drawn over others: it takes their look, named with the levels the game shows with it (its continue points and borrows in jak-project's goal_src) and the tasks that load it.
