# Jak 3: every level

Every level of Jak 3 the editor extracted (`library/jak3/<level>/`), 273 in all: 117 with decor of their own, the others loaded over them by the game (a mission's objects: 66; models only: 40; cutscenes: 17; characters for a scene: 22; menus and empty layers: 10).

Each row says what the level is and what sets it apart: the textures and objects it has that the other levels of its zone lack. Model for it with its zone's entry in [jak3.md](jak3.md). A level without decor of its own is drawn over others: it takes their look, named with the levels the game shows with it (its continue points and borrows in jak-project's goal_src) and the tasks that load it.
