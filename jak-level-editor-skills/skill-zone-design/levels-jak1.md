# Jak 1: every level

Every level of Jak 1 the decompiler wrote (`jak-project/decompiler_out/jak1/levels/`), 25 in all. Jak 1 is not in the editor's library: its textures are in `decompiler_out/jak1/textures/<level>-vis-*/`, its models in `decompiler_out/jak1/levels/<level>/`. Design for a level from its zone's entry in [jak1.md](jak1.md).
